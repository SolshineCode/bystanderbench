# BlueDot's recommended writing advice (Marius Hobbhahn, "Better posts", 2022), as a checklist
Source: https://www.mariushobbhahn.com/2022-03-07-better_posts/ (paraphrased).

1. Use visuals that carry information efficiently; simple figures are fine.
2. Order of priority: accurate, then understandable, then short.
3. Cut anything non-essential; push nuance to footnotes or an appendix instead of the main text.
4. State informed opinions plainly; don't fake balance between unequal arguments.
5. Use meaningful headings and some bold/italics so a skimmer finds the key points.
6. Put a summary at the top of anything longer than a page.
7. One central idea or question per post.
8. A plain, descriptive title that sets the right expectation.
9. Give numbers for confidence ("70%") instead of "probably".
10. Match background and jargon to the audience (here: BlueDot AI-safety course classmates and
    facilitators, technical but not all interpretability specialists).
11. Revise several times.
