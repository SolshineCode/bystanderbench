# Task for Antigravity: flow and naturalness review of the final write-up

Read final_draft_v3.md (below) as a first-time reader from the AI safety / frontier-lab community.

Part 1. An honest read, 5-8 short bullets: how natural it sounds (does it read like one specific
person, or like AI-assisted prose?), where the flow snags, where it drags, and any sentence that
still sounds artificial. Quote the exact sentences you mean.

Part 2. Up to 12 concrete edits that SHORTEN the text while improving flow and readability.
Priorities: cut repetition across sections (the same point made twice), cut process detail an
outside reader doesn't need, merge choppy sentences, tighten long ones. Rules:
- Keep every finding and every number that remains; don't make any result sound stronger.
- Keep the author's voice and the first-person "I".
- Follow 02_style_human_writing_check.md in full, including the newest tells: echoed frames
  ("it's the part X, and it's the part Y") and trailing kicker tags ("..., and that's the next test").
  No em dashes, no semicolons, no colon reveals, no stub sentences, no tricolons for rhythm.
- Don't add headings, and don't touch the figure lines ([[FIG:...]] and "Figure N." lines).

Format for Part 2, exactly:
  N.
  FIND: <exact text from the draft>
  REPLACE: <new text, or DELETE>
  WHY: <one line>
Print Part 1 first, then Part 2, nothing else.
