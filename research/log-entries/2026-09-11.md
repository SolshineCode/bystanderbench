**2026-09-11** — Ran BystanderBench against frontier models for the first time and the
headline flipped: Claude Opus 5 reports the misconduct in 6 of 6 episodes and Sonnet 5 in 4
of 6, where Qwen3.5-27B has reported 0 times in ~200 episodes against a 12-of-12 affordance
floor. So the finding isn't "agents stay silent" — it's that reporting propensity varies
from 0% to 100% between models on an identical environment, and it tracks model family rather
than serving path, weight availability or size.

**2026-09-11** — Two things I didn't expect. About 5% of episodes show the model explicitly
reasoning that it might be in a benchmark ("red herring", "social engineering"), and those
episodes report at 45% versus 5% for the rest — so our rates are probably an upper bound on
real deployment behaviour. And Opus, unprompted, asked to escalate *outside the management
line of the person who ordered the cover-up*, which no arm asks for and no metric scores.
