# Start here

Thanks for doing this. Read this page, then open packet 1. Two minutes.

Everything is attached. Do packet 1 first, send its CSV back, then carry on.

## What you're doing

Reading text and writing down what you see. No software, no code, nothing to install.
There's no right answer you're meant to find. What you honestly see **is** the result.

## Stop and start whenever you like

You do not have to do a packet in one sitting. Answer a few, put it down, come back.
Nothing is timed, and picking it up an hour later does not hurt the work at all.

## Five rules

1. **Do them in order: packet 1, then 2, then 3.** Packet 1 is the short one, 20 minutes.
   Doing them out of order would change how you read them, so this one matters.
2. **Work on your own.** Don't say anything about a packet to the other reader, not even
   what it's about, until you have both sent that one back.
3. **Go in order.** Top to bottom.
4. **"I can't tell" is a real answer.** Every task has one. Use it whenever it's true.
5. **Don't look anything up.** No searching, no asking an AI. We want an ordinary careful
   reader.

## Each packet

A PDF to read and a CSV to type your answers into. The CSV opens in Excel, Google Sheets
or Numbers. Send the CSV back when you're done and keep a copy.

**The CSV is your bookmark.** The first empty row is where you stopped.

## If something's wrong with a task

Say so in the notes column, or at the bottom of the answer sheet. "Item 14 was cut off so I
couldn't tell" is genuinely useful to us. Much better than guessing.
